About Our Dealership

We are a Mercedes-Benz dealership that focuses on electric vans. Every vehicle in our showroom is chosen for its design, electric engineering and reliability.

Our team works one-to-one with each customer to help them choose the right model, understand charging, and plan their daily routes.

From private buyers to companies with many vehicles, we give a complete experience: model walkthroughs, honest advice, and delivery support.

We believe luxury and sustainability can go together.
